# 🔋 Battery Swapping Station

> EV battery swapping station that replaces depleted batteries instead of charging, cutting wait time and carbon emissions.

## 📖 Overview

A charging station concept where vehicle batteries are **swapped** instead of recharged in place. A depleted battery is exchanged for a fully charged one, minimising the time a vehicle spends at the station and supporting lower carbon emissions.

## ❓ Problem

Long charging times are one of the biggest barriers to electric-vehicle adoption. Drivers can't wait hours for a full charge the way they'd wait minutes to refuel.

## ✨ Key Features

- Fast battery exchange instead of long charging sessions
- Charging of swapped-out batteries in the background
- Power-electronics design for safe, efficient charging
- Supports a cleaner, lower-emission transport ecosystem

## ⚙️ How It Works

1. Vehicle arrives with a depleted battery
2. Depleted battery is removed and a fully charged one is installed
3. Removed battery is placed on the station's charging system
4. Charged batteries are kept ready for the next vehicle
